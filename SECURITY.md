# Security policy

This policy applies to every Plenigon repository that does not provide its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately, by email to [security@plenigon.com](mailto:security@plenigon.com) or through the repository's **Report a vulnerability** button under **Security**, when it is enabled. Do not open public issues, discussions or pull requests for security problems.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- The affected repository, product or endpoint, and the version or commit.
- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, a proof of concept, or relevant logs.
- Any known mitigation.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We send an initial assessment within 10 business days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.
- We coordinate the disclosure date with you and credit you in the advisory unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Supported versions

Unless a repository states otherwise, only the latest release receives security fixes.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against research that is conducted in good faith, avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and is reported to us privately before any public disclosure.
